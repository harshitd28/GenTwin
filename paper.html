GenTwin: A Generative Industrial Immunosystem for Autonomous Resilience in Smart Manufacturing

Anirudh Pratap Singh Yadav · Harshit Divekar · Parth Shrivastava · Rajanikanth Aluvalu · Sai Prashanth Mallelu

Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Hyderabad Campus · Symbiosis International University, Pune

Download PDF
We present GenTwin, a five-layer generative industrial immunosystem that closes the loop from IIoT sensor anomaly to physics-validated recovery in 1,119 ± 121 ms. A two-stage ML gate (Random Forest + XGBoost) achieves 82.9% LLM bypass; RAG with faultCode-filtered ChromaDB retrieval and Layer 4 HAZOP validation yields zero violations across 100 trials versus unconstrained LLM targets up to 175 bar (34% above burst threshold).

1. Introduction

Industrial fault management remains alert-only: operators average 2,500+ seconds to diagnose and act. GenTwin models biological immune response across five non-redundant layers — Sense, Detect, Generate, Validate, Act.

2. Architecture

Layer 1 ingests OPC-UA telemetry at 100Hz averaged to 60s windows. Layer 2 Stage 1 RF screens in <12ms with 95% recall; Stage 2 XGBoost classifies HDF, PWF, OSF, TWF, RNF. Layer 3 five-agent swarm with Gemini 2.5 Flash. Layer 4 networkx KG + HAZOP. Layer 5 routes SOP, PLC, or deterministic shutdown.
